## Source routing

Classify the input before writing:

| Source | Strongest evidence | Completion standard |
|---|---|---|
| Video/audio link | full audiovisual source or sufficiently complete transcript | opening, middle, ending, and every substantive chapter covered |
| Subtitle/transcript | user-supplied or official full transcript | gaps and uncertain terms marked |
| News/article URL | canonical page plus cited primary sources | complete article inspected and material claims checked |
| Official announcement | canonical company/government/organization page | clearly labeled as the issuer's announcement |
| Supplied raw text | full text provided by user | scope limited to supplied text unless verification is requested |

Do not substitute search snippets, thumbnails, summaries, descriptions, comments, or memory for the source.

## Video and audio route

Use evidence in this order:

1. User-provided transcript or subtitle file.
2. Official platform captions or creator transcript.
3. Direct transcription of authorized user-provided media with an available tool.
4. Creator chapters and show notes as supporting metadata only.

Record the canonical URL, title, creator, publication date, duration, speakers, roles, chapters, caption language, and whether captions are auto-generated.

Build a chronological coverage map:

| Time range | Speaker | Topic | Claim/reasoning | Evidence/example | Caveat | Article destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use official chapters or 10–15 minute windows. Preserve examples, causal links, disagreement, uncertainty, and conditions. Mark `[听不清]`, `[说话人不确定]`, or `[术语待确认]` instead of guessing.

If no full audiovisual source or sufficiently complete transcript is available, do not claim complete整理. Request the video/audio file, `.srt`/`.vtt`, transcript export, or relevant clips.

## News and article route

Open the canonical page and record:

- exact headline, publisher, author/byline, publication date, and update date;
- article type: reporting, analysis, opinion, press release, company blog, official notice, or sponsored content;
- named people, roles, organizations, products, places, numbers, dates, and quotations;
- linked announcements, filings, reports, papers, datasets, documentation, or public records;
- which claims are the publisher's analysis rather than sourced facts.

Trace important claims to primary sources whenever practical. Prefer:

1. Government, regulator, court, exchange, or standards-body record.
2. Company or organization announcement and official documentation.
3. Original research paper, dataset, or named report.
4. Direct public statement, transcript, or recording.
5. Independent secondary reporting for context and disagreement.

Treat first-party metrics, customer cases, forecasts, and performance claims as issuer disclosures unless independently verified. Write `公司表示`, `官方案例显示`, or an equivalent attribution instead of presenting them as neutral findings.

If the page is inaccessible or incomplete, request the pasted article text or an accessible copy. Do not reconstruct it from search results.

## Supplied text route

Preserve the supplied text as the primary source. Clarify whether it is a complete article, notes, transcript, or draft. Verify current facts when requested or materially necessary, but keep external additions visibly separate from the supplied author's claims.

## Verification ledger

Maintain this internal table:

| Claim | Source wording | Claim type | Primary source | Status | Final treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use these claim types:

- `直接事实`: directly observable or documented fact.
- `引用`: statement attributed to a named person or organization.
- `发布方披露`: metric, result, or case disclosed by an interested party.
- `媒体观点`: interpretation or framing by the publisher.
- `外部核查`: independently checked fact from an authoritative source.

For every material name, role, date, number, quotation, and company action, mark one of:

- `confirmed`;
- `confirmed with qualification`;
- `unverified`;
- `contradicted`.

Do not silently repair a source claim. If authoritative evidence contradicts it, report the difference and preserve attribution.

## Attribution and copyright

Prefer paraphrase and short quotations. Do not reproduce long copyrighted passages or deliver a third-party video's full verbatim transcript. A transformative structured summary and original article are the default.

Keep image provenance. For official, press, scientific, or editorial images, state the direct source and any known licensing or reuse requirement. Do not imply that publicly accessible means unrestricted reuse.

## Failure states

State limitations explicitly when:

- the source page is blocked, paywalled, deleted, or only partially accessible;
- video captions cover only part of the runtime;
- audio is inaudible or speakers cannot be identified;
- the publication date, author, quotation, or number cannot be verified;
- only a company claim exists and no independent verification was found;
- image rights are unclear.

Report what was accessed, what remains missing, and the smallest user action needed to continue accurately.
